# Wedsent visual reset — rejected prototypes, real reference atlas and new creative assignment

**Status: owner-rejected visual designs, 2026-11-07.** The first and second websites are not approved. Their page structures, typography choices, colours, banners, invitation compositions and onboarding visual treatments are **not design precedents**. They remain functional scaffolding only. Do not preserve, lightly reskin, or treat them as product quality benchmarks.

## Why both attempts failed

- Formulaic one-hero / repeating section banner / three-card gallery / steps strip / feature list / FAQ / giant CTA composition; looks produced by generic AI page recipe.
- No bespoke campaign world, no original illustrations, photography, art-directed assets or cinematic visual narrative.
- Typography alone carried every section; little visual variety, poor pacing and identical component motifs.
- Invitation designs were effectively CSS poster exercises without their own complete photographic, editorial, interactive and cultural art directions.
- Incomplete content: a list of product claims and placeholders instead of real interactive demonstrations, clear explanations and expressive rich showcases.
- Studio felt like a standard question form beside a phone card, not a crafted, responsive creation experience.
- Unverified creative quality: passing `next build` is not visual QA. No documented mobile screenshots, human comparison or scene-by-scene revisions.
- "Trust" cannot be created through a FAQ alone; genuine trust comes from authentic product demonstrations, transparent terms, high craft and consistent operating details.

## Art direction is not a palette choice

Before producing the next homepage, make **three substantial creative routes**. Each route must show original hero composition, real invitation reveal, navigation/mobile frame, two richly developed downstream content scenes, Studio entrance, collection detail page and real typography specimens. Explain its distinctive *concept*, asset production plan and UX implications.

### Route A — THE FIRST MOMENT

The invitation as a crafted object. The opening feels like a reveal in a short film. Hero should be a richly art-directed digital invitation floating in a real atmosphere, but user can interact with it. Materials could include translucent vellum, foil-like microtypography, photographic collage or letterpress-inspired depth. Visual content evolves as the invitation opens. Critically: no generic mock iPhone-in-beige-box and no permanently hidden details.

### Route B — LOVE, IN FULL COLOUR

Contemporary youth-culture visual energy. Collection artwork, expressive typography, independently directed graphics, candid editorial photography and deliberately off-grid composition. Different celebrations deserve different voices; the Wedsent brand remains a coherent frame. Avoid default orange-and-lilac 2026 AI gradients and stock couple portraits.

### Route C — THE INVITATION HOUSE

A high-design, almost gallery-like studio. Deep modern editorial control, large visual surfaces, quiet navigation, beautifully curated type and contrast, unexpected visual transitions. Every collection has a small self-contained case study with hero scene, motion, texture, typography and real guest view. Not monochrome cards and thin lines repeated all the way down.

**Do not code a full third homepage until at least one route has been rendered as a genuine, comprehensive visual design and inspected.** The routes may be combined selectively, but each must be distinct enough to support comparison. User taste has rejected both previous AI-generated compositions; this is a meaningful creative blocker, not an arbitrary feature approval gate.

## Required content architecture (real depth, varied composition)

This is a content inventory, **not a vertical sequence of identical banners**. Art director chooses placement and storytelling transitions. At least half the modules must be visually expressed rather than simply explained by headings.

1. **Signature interactive opening:** one real premium invitation composition with working opening state, clearly visible core CTA and skip/reduced-motion option.
2. **Design collection stories:** 3–4 fully designed visual worlds initially; each with distinct imagery/illustration, material, entrance, typography, guest view, content resilience and customisation demonstrations. Independent visual compositions, not cards or background substitutions.
3. **Make it yours:** a split or immersive view where edited names, ceremony, type, palette and photos produce honest in-place changes. Demonstrate variation, not a list of settings.
4. **From your phone to theirs:** visually explain share and arrival in WhatsApp, actual OG metadata, named invitation opening and no-account guest RSVP; feature labels reflect shipped state.
5. **Multiple celebrations:** an authentic complex scenario with Nikah, Walima and reception or civil/cultural alternatives; explicitly show guests invited to different events. Avoid presenting cultures as theme categories.
6. **People, not spreadsheets:** show useful household RSVP summary and adjustment flow with actual product UI once backed by data, without pretending an unauthenticated demo is live.
7. **The practical moments:** maps, ceremony details, dietary notes, reminders, changes, RSVP cutoff and manual fallback explained through thoughtfully designed contextual content.
8. **Photo Hall, when working:** photographer upload → couple approval → private guest albums. Could be a memorable post-wedding emotional story, but don't headline before shipping.
9. **Transparent purchase and privacy:** one-price explanation, hosting length, limits, who can view data, cancellation/expiry, rights and support; no fake testimonials, figures or press logos.
10. **Mobile-first journey and Studio:** not a screenshot-only product; real interactive maker with 5-minute intended draft flow, contextual inputs, full-screen preview, keyboard handling and no blank actions.

## Design delivery and QA contract

- Produce visual reference contact sheets with sources and analysis, then three route concepts shown as **complete scenes**, including mobile. No more abstract prose-only 'directions'.
- Prefer original vector/3D/bitmap assets and legitimate photographic treatments with proper rights records. AI-generated art must be reviewed at full resolution; no 'AI slop' because it is fast.
- Typography pairing must be selected by optical evidence and sample copy, not just a trendy font name. Test names, Arabic, multi-event content and legibility.
- Audit motion, transitions, layout and interaction by actual browser screenshots/recording on desktop and mobile. Compare against the references and reject formulaic sequences.
- Build rich pages only after assets, scene storyboards, responsive compositions and product data contracts are in place. Then code directly into Wedsent and verify.
- The user wants active building. Research should directly yield executable design components, assets and a tested interface. Avoid replacing coding with endless theoretical documents.
- Do not claim the product is '10%, 50%' etc. complete without actual functional coverage evidence. Current design is not approved, backend and commercial launch are missing.

## Creative anti-pattern ban

- Same rectangular banner at the start of every section.
- Three symmetrical feature cards used to explain every concept.
- Text-only blocks claiming 'beautiful' instead of actually displaying the product.
- Repetitive uppercase eyebrow + huge italic heading + paragraph + CTA pattern.
- Excessive em-dash/dual-hyphen AI copy, emojis or forced slang.
- Generic AI textures, random gradients, stock wedding couples as gallery stand-ins.
- Fake polish: mock UI claiming data operations that do not exist.
- A full homepage coded before anyone can see and assess what its creative direction actually looks like.
